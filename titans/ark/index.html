ARK Invest holdings · latest filing

Cathie Wood founded ARK Invest in 2014. It looks for companies it expects to be changed by new technology — artificial intelligence, robotics, energy storage, genomics, blockchain — and holds them mostly through actively managed ETFs. It spreads its money across many names rather than a few large bets, and the share counts of nearly every holding move from one quarter to the next. ARK publishes its fund holdings daily; this page uses only the quarterly 13F, so it can be read on the same terms as the other investors here. The filings run from 2016.
