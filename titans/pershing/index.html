Pershing Square holdings · latest filing

Bill Ackman runs Pershing Square, an activist fund: it takes large stakes in a small number of companies — often around ten — and presses management for changes it believes will raise their value. The portfolio is concentrated, so a single decision moves it a lot. A 13F shows only its US-listed shares; options and anything held outside that scope are left out. The filings run from 2013. They came from the fund manager until early 2026 and from the group’s holding company since then; for the four quarters in 2025–26 when both filed, the two reports are added together here.
