Appaloosa holdings · latest filing

David Tepper traded high-yield and distressed debt at Goldman Sachs before founding Appaloosa in 1993. He is known for buying what others are fleeing — most famously the battered bank stocks of early 2009. A 13F shows only the stock side of that approach: bonds and most options are left out, so this page covers part of the portfolio. The filings run from 2013; the firm changed legal entity in 2016, and because the same officer signed both and most holdings carried over, the two filing histories are joined here.
