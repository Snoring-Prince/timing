Duquesne Family Office holdings · latest filing

Stanley Druckenmiller founded Duquesne Capital in 1981 and, from 1988 to 2000, also ran George Soros's Quantum Fund. In 2010 he returned his clients' money and has since invested his own through this family office. He is a macro investor: his biggest bets are often on interest rates, currencies and commodities, made through bonds and futures that a 13F does not show. This page covers only the shares it reports, which change a great deal from quarter to quarter. The filings run from 2013.
